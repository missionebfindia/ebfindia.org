What is exclusive breastfeeding?

Exclusive breastfeeding means the baby receives breast milk only. WHO recommends it for the first six months of life, then adding appropriate complementary foods while breastfeeding continues up to two years or beyond.

Every family's situation is different. If you have questions about feeding, medicines, milk supply or your baby's health, please speak with a qualified doctor, nurse or lactation professional.

In the first six months
Breast milk only. No other milk, food or water.
When medically needed
Oral rehydration solution, and vitamin, mineral or medicine drops and syrups, are still compatible with exclusive breastfeeding.
From six months
Appropriate complementary foods are introduced, and breastfeeding continues.

What EBF India does
